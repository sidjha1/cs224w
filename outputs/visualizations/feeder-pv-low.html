Bus = electrical connection point. Customer buses connect demand; junctions connect wires. A customer bus can aggregate multiple loads.

Transformers are edges. Their terminal buses are the connection points on either side. They step distribution voltage down to customer service voltage. LL means line-to-line; a split-phase service has two 120 V legs.

HC = additional solar output in kW. Each label adds solar at that customer alone, with other customers at baseline. Unlabeled buses do not have zero capacity. Highlighted paths show connectivity, not power-flow direction or geographic distance.
